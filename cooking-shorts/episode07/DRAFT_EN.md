## Full recipe

1 serving / about 25 min + preheating

Salmon 150 g / 1 bell pepper (about 150 g)

Oil 1 tsp / pepper to taste / salt optional

1 Preheat to 200°C (392°F). Pat fish dry; check bones.

2 Deseed pepper and cut into 1 cm strips.

3 Arrange on oven-safe tray; coat with oil and pepper.

4 Bake about 15–20 min; fish center must reach 63°C.

145°F internal / adjust time to thickness / thaw first
