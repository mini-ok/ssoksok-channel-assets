Chef Immune here. Today we are making chocolate flavored sunflower seed bites.

Get unsalted roasted sunflower seeds, soft dates, unsweetened cocoa, and water.

Finely grind most of the seeds. Take the pits out of the soft dates and mash them with a fork.

Mix the ground seeds and dates with cocoa and water. Stir until it holds together.

Roll the mixture into three balls. Crush the remaining seeds and coat the outside.

Chill for twenty minutes to firm up. Crisp outside and chewy inside.

Sunflower seeds supply vitamin E. Vitamin E supports normal immune function and helps protect cells from oxidative damage.

The amount shown counts all the sunflower seeds in three bites. One bite provides about a third of the amount shown.

Roll them round, and your snack is ready to roll!
