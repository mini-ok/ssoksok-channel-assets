Today Chef Eye is making golden sweet potato boats. Let us talk about vitamin A.

Get one sweet potato, olive oil, and ground cumin.

Scrub and prick the potato. Bake it whole at two hundred degrees Celsius until soft.

Cut it in half and scoop out the flesh. Mash it with cumin and olive oil.

Fill the skins with the mash. Bake again at the same temperature until golden on top.

Golden on top and fluffy inside. Eat the skin and filling together.

Your body can turn beta carotene from sweet potatoes into vitamin A. The retina needs vitamin A to detect light.

The number is a reference for one sweet potato baked in its skin. The actual amount varies by size and variety.
