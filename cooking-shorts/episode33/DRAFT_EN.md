Chef Immune here. Today we are making tangy Brussels sprouts.

Get Brussels sprouts, olive oil, cider vinegar, and mustard.

Wash and trim the sprouts. Cut them in half and slice thinly.

Pour vinegar into a small bowl. Stir in the mustard.

Cook the sprouts in the skillet. When the edges turn golden, add the sauce and let it reduce briefly.

Serve when the leaves are tender and the edges are lightly crisp.

Brussels sprouts supply vitamin C. Your immune system needs vitamin C to work normally.

The estimate is for cooked sprouts alone. The actual amount can vary with cooking method and time.
