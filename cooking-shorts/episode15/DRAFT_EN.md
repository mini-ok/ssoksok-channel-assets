Chef Gut reporting! Let us make a cozy porridge with cooked barley and pumpkin!

Get cooked barley, cooked pumpkin, and water. The amounts are on screen.

Mash the cooked, peeled pumpkin with a fork until smooth.

Put the pumpkin and water in a saucepan. Add the cooked barley and stir well.

Once it starts bubbling, lower the heat. Stir the bottom and simmer until thick.

Serve in a bowl. Add a splash of water if the porridge gets too thick.
