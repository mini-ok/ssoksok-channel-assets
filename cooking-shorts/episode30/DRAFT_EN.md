Chef Immune here. Today we are making a crunchy mango ice dessert.

Get a ripe mango, lime juice, and cold water.

Remove the skin and pit. Cut the mango flesh into small cubes.

Blend the mango with lime juice and water until smooth. Pour it into a shallow dish.

Put it in the freezer. Scrape with a fork when the edges freeze, and repeat as it sets.

When small ice crystals form, spoon them into a chilled glass and serve right away.

Mango supplies vitamin C. Your immune system needs vitamin C to work normally.

The estimate is for raw mango before freezing. The amount can vary by variety and storage.
