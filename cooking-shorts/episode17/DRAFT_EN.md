Chef Bone reporting! Let us warm some tofu and add a little ginger!

Get firm tofu, soy sauce and ginger. Check the tofu ingredients for calcium sulfate.

Drain the tofu and slice it into bite sized pieces.

Put the tofu on a microwave safe plate and warm it according to the package directions.

Mix the soy sauce and grated ginger. Spoon it lightly over the warm tofu.

Soft tofu and fragrant ginger! Enjoy this little plate while it is warm.

Calcium helps form the structure of bones and teeth. Tofu made with calcium sulfate can provide calcium.

The amount shown is for firm tofu made with calcium sulfate. Different coagulants and products can change the amount.

Soft tofu, sturdy spirit! Chef Bone is ready to serve!
