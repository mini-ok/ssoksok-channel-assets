Chef Immune here. Let us poach shrimp in a fragrant tomato broth.

Get peeled shrimp, tomato, garlic, olive oil, and water.

Chop the tomato and mince the garlic.

Stir the garlic in oil over low heat. Add the tomato and water and bring to a gentle simmer.

When the tomato softens, add the shrimp. Cook until the flesh is firm and opaque throughout.

Spoon the shrimp into a bowl with some of the tomato broth.

Shrimp supply selenium. Your body uses this mineral to protect cells from oxidative damage and support immune function.
