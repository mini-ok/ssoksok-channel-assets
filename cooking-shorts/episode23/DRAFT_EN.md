Chef Muscle here. Let us make crisp black bean and corn patties.

Get cooked black beans, cooked corn, oat flour, and olive oil.

Drain the beans and mash them with a fork. Leave a few whole for texture.

Mix in the corn and oat flour. Divide in two and flatten with your palms.

Heat oil in a skillet over medium heat. Flip the patties when the bottoms are firm.

Take them out once both sides are golden. Let them rest briefly to firm up.

Black beans supply magnesium. Your body needs magnesium for normal muscle contraction and nerve signaling.
