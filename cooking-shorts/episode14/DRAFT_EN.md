Use twenty eight grams of dry roasted unsalted almonds, one slice of whole wheat bread, fifty grams of pear, and a little water.

Grind almonds finely in a small blender. Add water gradually to make a moist spread.

Wash, core and thinly slice the pear. Lightly toast the bread in a toaster.

Spread the almond mixture evenly on the toast. Arrange the pear slices on top!

Cut into manageable pieces and eat promptly. Grind almonds finely without large chunks.
