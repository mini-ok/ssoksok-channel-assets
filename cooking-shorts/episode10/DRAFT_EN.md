Chef Eye: Cook pasta according to its package. Cook the frozen spinach as directed, drain it, then measure!

## 전체 레시피 / Full recipe

1 serving / about 20 minutes

Dry pasta 80 g / cooked frozen spinach 1/2 US cup

Garlic 1 clove / oil 2 tsp / pasta water ~3 tbsp

1 Cook pasta as directed; reserve 3 tbsp cooking water.

2 Cook frozen spinach as directed; drain and measure.

3 Gently warm sliced garlic in oil on low ~30 sec.

4 Toss pasta, spinach and water on medium-low ~1 min.

US measuring cup / season to taste / do not burn garlic
