Chef Gut here. Let us make crispy little artichoke bites.

Get cooked artichoke hearts, breadcrumbs, olive oil, and lemon juice.

Pat the hearts dry. Cut them in half for bite sized pieces.

Coat the hearts with lemon juice. Mix the oil into the crumbs and coat the outside.

Space the pieces out on a tray. Bake until the crumbs turn golden.

Serve warm. The coating is crisp and the center is tender.
