Get a whole piece of beef, lettuce, olive oil, and cracked black pepper.

Mix the olive oil and black pepper in a small bowl.

Rub the pepper oil evenly over the outside of the beef.

Roast until the center reaches at least sixty three degrees Celsius. Let it rest for at least three minutes before cutting.

Slice the beef thinly across the grain. Put it on washed lettuce and fold the sides in.
