Chef Brain here. Today we are making warm clam and potato soup.

Get cooked clam meat, a potato, garlic, olive oil, and water.

Peel the potato and cut it into small cubes.

Stir garlic in oil over low heat. When fragrant, add the potato and water.

When the potato is tender, mash half of it. Add the cooked clams and heat the soup through.

Ladle into a bowl. Each spoonful has tender potato and clams.

Clams supply vitamin B twelve. Your central nervous system, including the brain, needs it to function normally.

The estimate uses cooked clam meat without shells. Amounts can vary by species.

A little mashing makes the whole soup silky.
