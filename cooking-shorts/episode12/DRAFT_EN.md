# Milk Chia Pudding

## hook
Chef Bone here! Make milk chia pudding without a stove, with calcium that helps form bone structure!

## ingredients
Use one US cup of nonfat milk, three tablespoons of chia seeds, and a little vanilla. Maple syrup is optional.

## prep
Put milk and chia seeds in a bowl. Add vanilla and stir until evenly mixed.

## mix
Rest for five minutes, then stir again along the bottom and sides. Break up seed clumps.

## cook
Cover and refrigerate for at least four hours. Let the seeds absorb liquid and swell fully!

## serve
Divide the thickened pudding equally between two cups. Do not swallow dry chia seeds.

## benefit
Calcium is a building material in bones. Milk and chia seeds can add calcium to a varied diet.
