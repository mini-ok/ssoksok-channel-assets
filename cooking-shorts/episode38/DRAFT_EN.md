Chef Immune here. Let us make tender pears with a crunchy nut topping.

Get dry roasted hazelnuts, pear, and cinnamon.

Remove the pear core and slice the flesh thinly. Put it in a small oven dish.

Toss the pear with cinnamon and bake it first. Meanwhile, coarsely crush the hazelnuts.

When the pear is tender, scatter the nuts over it. Bake a little longer until fragrant.

Cool briefly and serve. Each spoonful has soft pear and crisp hazelnuts.

Hazelnuts supply vitamin E. Vitamin E supports normal immune function and helps protect cells from oxidative damage.

The estimate counts only dry roasted hazelnuts. The pear and cinnamon are excluded.

Soft pear and crunchy nuts. One spoon can do it all!
