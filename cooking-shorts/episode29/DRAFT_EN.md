Chef Muscle here. Let us make tender steamed dumplings with edamame.

Get cooked shelled edamame, wrappers, ginger, and soy sauce.

Drain the beans and mash with a fork. Leave some small pieces for texture.

Mix in ginger and soy sauce. Divide among the wrappers, wet the edges, and press to seal.

Put the dumplings in a hot steamer with space between them. Steam until the wrappers are tender.

Plate the dumplings. The tender wrapper gives way to little pieces of edamame.
