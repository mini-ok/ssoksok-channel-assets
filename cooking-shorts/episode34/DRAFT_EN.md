Chef Bone here. Let us make a quesadilla with melted cheese.

Get part skim mozzarella, a tortilla, and a tomato.

Dice the tomato and pat it dry. Cut the cheese into small pieces.

Spread cheese and tomato over half of the tortilla. Fold the other half over the filling.

Cook in a dry skillet over medium low heat. Flip when golden and cook until the cheese melts.

Rest briefly and cut into three pieces. Crisp outside and soft inside.

Cheese supplies calcium. Calcium is a mineral your body uses to build the structure of bones and teeth.

The estimate counts the part skim mozzarella alone. Calcium varies by product, so check the package label.

Fold it up, and the cheese has a little home!
