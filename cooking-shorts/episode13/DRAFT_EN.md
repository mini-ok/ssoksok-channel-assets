Chef Muscle reporting! Kidney beans bring potassium for normal muscle contraction!

Prepare one US cup of ready to eat canned kidney beans, fifty grams of cucumber, lemon juice, and oil.

Wash and dice cucumber. Use ready to eat canned kidney beans, not raw dried beans.

Put beans and cucumber in a bowl. Add one tablespoon of lemon juice and one teaspoon of olive oil.

Lift and fold gently with a spoon. Toss lightly so the beans keep their shape!

Serve in one bowl. Skip added salt and let the fresh lemon flavor shine.

Potassium is needed for nerve signals and normal muscle contraction. Kidney beans can add potassium to a varied diet.

The figures use a standard canned bean value. Follow personal dietary advice for kidney disease or a potassium restriction.

Muscle needs a gentle touch! Do not turn this salad into mashed beans!
