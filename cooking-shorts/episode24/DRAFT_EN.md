Chef Bone here. Golden mushrooms are getting a silky lemon sauce.

Get UV treated white mushrooms, milk, flour, olive oil, and lemon juice.

Wipe the mushrooms dry and cut thick slices. Wide flat sides are easy to brown.

Whisk the flour into cold milk until smooth.

Brown the mushrooms in oil. Add the milk mixture and stir over low heat until thick.

Turn off the heat when the sauce thickens. Stir in the lemon juice and serve.

UV treated white mushrooms can supply vitamin D. Vitamin D helps your body absorb calcium.

The estimate uses raw white mushrooms exposed to ultraviolet light. Amounts vary, so check the vitamin D on the package.
