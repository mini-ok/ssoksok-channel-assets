Chef Bone here. Let us make crisp chips from kale leaves.

Get kale, olive oil, and smoked paprika.

Wash and dry the kale thoroughly. Remove the tough stems and tear the leaves.

Mix the oil and smoked paprika. Rub a thin coat onto the leaves.

Spread the leaves in one layer. Bake at low heat until the edges are crisp.

Take the tray out and let the chips cool. They crisp up as they cool.

Kale supplies vitamin K. Vitamin K helps proteins in your bones do their job.

The estimate is for raw kale before baking. If you take warfarin, keeping your vitamin K intake consistent is important.
