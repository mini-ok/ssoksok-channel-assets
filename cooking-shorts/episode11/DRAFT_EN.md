# Pumpkin Seed Rice Balls

## hook
Chef Immune reporting! Pumpkin seeds bring zinc, a nutrient needed for normal immune function!

## ingredients
Use one hundred fifty grams of cooked brown rice, twenty eight grams of roasted unsalted pumpkin seeds, and one teaspoon of sesame oil.

## prep
Finely chop the pumpkin seeds on a board. Break up any large pieces.

## mix
Mix the chopped seeds and sesame oil evenly into the warm cooked rice.

## cook
Wet clean hands with water. Divide the rice in half and gently press into two rice balls!

## serve
Take small bites and chew well. Enjoy the rice balls warm, soon after making them.

## benefit
Zinc helps immune cells function normally. Pumpkin seeds are one way to include zinc in a varied diet.

## context
The figures count only the roasted seeds. Not all the zinc eaten is absorbed, and this meal does not guarantee cold prevention.
