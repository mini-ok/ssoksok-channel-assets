Chef Muscle reporting! Let us grill a chicken breast and finish it with lemon!

Get skinless boneless chicken breast, lemon juice, and cooking oil.

Flatten the chicken to an even thickness. Brush lightly with oil and wash tools that touched raw meat.

Put the chicken on a hot grill pan. Turn it with tongs and cook both sides evenly.

Use a thermometer to check that the thickest part reaches seventy four degrees Celsius.

Transfer to a clean plate and add lemon juice. Serve eighty five grams of cooked chicken.

Potassium is needed for nerve signals and normal muscle contraction. Grilled chicken can contribute potassium.

The numbers are for the cooked meat. Follow your own dietary advice if you need to limit potassium.
