Chef Immune here. We are making fragrant broccoli with vitamin C.

Get broccoli, olive oil, garlic, and lemon juice.

Cut the broccoli into small florets. Steam for three to four minutes.

Add oil and garlic to a pan. Stir over medium low heat until fragrant.

Add the steamed broccoli and toss for about a minute to coat it with garlic oil.

Turn off the heat and toss with lemon juice. Plate while still crisp.

Broccoli supplies vitamin C. Your immune system needs vitamin C to work normally.

This reference counts cooked broccoli alone. Vitamin C is sensitive to heat, so the amount varies with cooking method and time.
