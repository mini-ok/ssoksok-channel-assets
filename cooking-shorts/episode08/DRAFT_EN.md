Chef Bone: Wash the strawberries and pat them dry! Slice thinly so they are easier to eat after freezing.

## Full recipe

2 snack servings / prep 10 min + freeze 3–4+ hr

Unsweetened plain low-fat yogurt 200 g

Strawberries 80 g (about 5 medium berries)

1 Wash, dry, hull, and thinly slice strawberries.

2 Spread yogurt 5–7 mm thick on parchment.

3 Press in berries; cover; freeze 3–4+ hr until firm.

4 Soften 2–5 min; divide into small pieces.

Wait longer if too hard / keep leftovers frozen
