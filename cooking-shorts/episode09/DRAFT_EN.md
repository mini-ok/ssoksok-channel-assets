# 9 · Lentil Onion Soup

## hook · A Warm Bowl from Chef Gut

Chef Gut: Chef Gut is warming things up today! Lentil soup brings fiber to the table!

## ingredients · Start with Precooked Lentils

여성 해설 / Narrator: One cup of cooked lentils, half an onion, and three hundred fifty milliliters of water. Add a teaspoon of oil.

## prep · 01 Finely Chop the Onion

Chef Gut: Finely chop the onion. Small pieces soften quickly in our soup!

## mix · 02 Soften the Onion First

여성 해설 / Narrator: Add oil to a pot. Soften the onion over medium-low heat for about three minutes.

## cook · 03 Add Lentils and Simmer

Chef Gut: Add the cooked lentils and water! Bring to a boil, then simmer gently for about seven minutes.

## serve · Divide into Two Bowls

여성 해설 / Narrator: Once the onion is tender, divide into two bowls. Season lightly with salt and pepper if you like.

## benefit · Why Gut Chose Lentils

여성 해설 / Narrator: Lentils provide fiber. Fiber and enough fluids help soften stools and support easier bowel movements.

## context · How Much Fiber in One Bowl?

여성 해설 / Narrator: Increase fiber gradually rather than all at once. Remember to drink water too!

## 전체 레시피 / Full recipe

2 bowls / about 15 min / precooked lentils

Cooked lentils 1 US cup (~200 g) / onion 100 g

Water 350 mL / oil 1 tsp / salt and pepper optional

1 Finely chop onion; use fully cooked lentils.

2 Add oil; soften onion on medium-low about 3 min.

3 Add lentils and water; boil, then simmer ~7 min.

4 Once onion is tender, season and divide into 2 bowls.

US measuring cup / dry-lentil cooking time is extra
