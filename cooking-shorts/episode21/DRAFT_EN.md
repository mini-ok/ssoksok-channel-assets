Chef Gut here. Today we are making a tart raspberry compote.

Get raspberries, water, lemon juice, and ground cinnamon.

Rinse and drain the raspberries. Put them in a small saucepan.

Crush half the berries with a fork. Add water and cinnamon.

Simmer gently and stir. When a spoon leaves a brief trail on the bottom, turn off the heat.

Stir in lemon juice. Serve the whole berries with their thick sauce.

Raspberries supply dietary fiber. Getting enough fluid along with fiber can help keep stools soft and easier to pass.
