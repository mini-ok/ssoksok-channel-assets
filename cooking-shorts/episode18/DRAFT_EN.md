Chef Brain reporting! Let us give a boiled egg a little color with paprika!

Get one large egg, paprika powder, and enough water for cooking.

Put the egg in a saucepan and add enough cold water to cover it.

Bring the water to a boil, lower the heat, and cook until the white and yolk are firm.

Cool in cold water, peel the shell, and cut the egg in half to check it is firm.

Dust the yolks evenly with paprika and the dish is ready!
