Chef Eye here. Let us make crisp carrot ribbon pickles.

Get a carrot, rice vinegar, water, ginger, and sugar.

Wash the carrot and shave it into long ribbons. Place them in a heatproof bowl.

Mix the vinegar and water with ginger and sugar. Warm it while stirring.

Pour the warm brine over the carrot. Toss once, cool, then let it pickle in the fridge.

Lift out the ribbons when they taste tangy. Serve them cold.

Carrots contain beta carotene, which your body can turn into vitamin A. The retina needs vitamin A to detect light.

This estimate uses raw carrots before pickling. It can vary by variety and the amount used.

These carrot ribbons are dressed for a tangy dinner.
