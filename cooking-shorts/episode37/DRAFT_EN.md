Chef Brain here. Let us bake cod with lemon and parsley.

Get Atlantic cod, lemon zest, chopped parsley, and olive oil.

Gently pat the surface of the cod dry with a paper towel.

Mix the lemon zest and parsley with oil. Spread evenly over the top of the cod.

Bake in a preheated oven. Remove when the thickest part reaches sixty three degrees Celsius.

Transfer the cod to a plate. A fork lifts the cooked fish in flakes.

Cod supplies choline. Your body uses it to make a chemical messenger for the brain and nervous system.

The estimate counts only baked Atlantic cod. Other kinds of fish can have different amounts.
