Chef Brain here. Let us turn sardines into a creamy garlic dip.

Get sardines canned in tomato sauce, garlic, olive oil, and lemon juice.

Drain the sardines in a strainer. Put the drained fish in a bowl.

Add oil and garlic to a skillet. Stir over low heat until the garlic turns pale gold.

Turn off the heat. Add the garlic oil to the sardines. Mash with a fork and stir in lemon juice.

Spoon the thick dip into a dish. Try it with cucumber or whole wheat bread.

Sardines supply DHA, a type of omega three fat. DHA is part of the membranes that surround brain cells.

The estimate is for sardines drained from tomato sauce. It varies by product. There is no separate United States daily value for this fatty acid.
