Chef Gut here. Let us make a bright salad with cracked whole wheat.

Get bulgur, parsley, cucumber, lemon juice, and olive oil.

Cook the bulgur as directed on its package. Spread it out on a plate to release steam.

Chop the parsley and finely dice the cucumber. Whisk lemon juice with olive oil.

Fluff the cooled bulgur with a fork. Toss with the cucumber, parsley, and lemon dressing.

Spoon it into a bowl. Each bite has tender grains, crisp cucumber, and fresh herbs.

Bulgur supplies dietary fiber. Getting enough fiber and fluids can help keep stools soft and easier to pass.
