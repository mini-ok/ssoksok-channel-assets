1. 어떤 앱인가요

이 앱은 고엑스온이 운영하는 YouTube 채널 "쏙쏙 건강백과"와 "Tiny Body Co."에 영상을 올리기 위한 개인용 도구입니다. 채널 소유자 본인만 사용하며, 일반 사용자를 대상으로 하지 않습니다.

소유자가 구글 로그인 화면에서 직접 승인한 범위 안에서만, 소유자 본인 소유 YouTube 채널에 대해 다음 일을 합니다.

  • 소유자 채널 영상에 댓글 작성
  • 채널 정보 조회(올바른 채널에 올리는지 확인하기 위한 채널 이름과 주소)

요청하는 권한 범위(OAuth scope)는 https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube와 https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl입니다.

Privacy Policy

1. About this app

Ssoksok Video Uploader is a personal tool operated by GOXON to upload videos to its YouTube channels "쏙쏙 건강백과" and "Tiny Body Co." It is used only by the channel owner and is not offered to the public.

2. Information the app accesses

Only within the scope the owner explicitly authorizes on Google's consent screen, and only for YouTube channels owned by the owner, the app:

  • uploads videos and sets video details (title, description, tags, category, visibility);
  • sets thumbnails, and creates playlists and adds videos to them;
  • posts a comment on the owner's own videos;
  • reads channel information (channel name and URL) to confirm that a video goes to the correct channel.

OAuth scopes requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl.

3. Collection, storage, use and sharing

  • The app does not collect, store or use anyone else's personal data. It does not access viewers' information or other users' YouTube data.
  • OAuth tokens (refresh tokens) issued during authorization are stored only in the owner's private environment settings and are used only to perform uploads.
  • No information is sold, shared or transferred to third parties, and none is used for advertising or analytics.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube API Services through this app is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Revoking access and deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time on the Google Account permissions page. After revocation the stored tokens can no longer be used, and the owner deletes them from the environment settings.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
